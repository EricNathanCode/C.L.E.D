# C.L.I.D – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

C.L.I.D (CLID: A Comic-Based Learning Interactive for Database Management System) is a free educational game for practicing SQL, made as a student capstone project at Cavite State University.

## What the game saves (on your device only)
- Your username and password
- Your lesson progress, stars, Folder Challenge results and Simulation best scores
- Your settings (music, sound effects, text-to-speech, dark mode)

## What the game does NOT do
- It does not send any of your information to us or to anyone else. It has no online accounts, no ads and no tracking.
- Text-to-speech uses the voices already built into your computer or browser.

## Your password
Your account exists only on your device. Your password is saved on the device without encryption, so do not use a password that you use anywhere else. We cannot recover a forgotten password or a lost account.

## Where your data is stored
- Windows (Microsoft Store or download): in your user folder, under AppData\Roaming\Godot\app_userdata\C.L.E.D
- Linux: in your home folder, under .local/share/Godot/app_userdata/C.L.E.D
- Web version: in your browser's storage for the itch.io website. The itch.io website is run by itch.io and follows its own privacy policy.

## Removing your data
- Settings → Delete My Account (while logged in) permanently removes your account, progress, stars and Simulation scores.
- Settings on the Login screen → Delete All Data removes every account and all progress on the device and resets all settings.
- Microsoft Store version: uninstall C.L.I.D from the Start menu (right-click → Uninstall). Use Delete All Data first if you also want your saved data gone.

## Younger players
If you are under 18, please play with the guidance of a parent, guardian or teacher.
