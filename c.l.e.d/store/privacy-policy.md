# CLID – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

CLID: A Comic-Based Learning Interactive for Database Management System is a free educational game for practicing SQL, made as a student capstone project at Cavite State University.

## What the game saves (on your device only)
- Your username and password
- Your lesson progress, stars, Folder Challenge results and Simulation best scores
- Your settings (music, sound effects, text-to-speech, dark mode, Class Ranking sharing) and a random code that tells your computer apart in the Class Ranking

## What the game does NOT do
- It does not send any of your information to us or over the internet. It has no online accounts, no ads and no tracking. The only thing it shares is the Class Ranking below, with players on your own Wi-Fi.
- Text-to-speech uses the voices already built into your computer or browser.

## Class Ranking (local network only)
- While you are logged in, the Windows and Linux apps share your username, your lesson stars and your Simulation best scores with other CLID players on the same Wi-Fi or local network, so classmates can see the ranking. Your password is never shared.
- This stays on your local network: it is not sent over the internet or to us. Anyone on the same network who runs CLID can see these scores.
- To stop sharing, open Rank on the lessons page and untick "Share my name and scores with classmates on this Wi-Fi".
- The web version does not share anything.

## Your password
Your account exists only on your device. Your password is saved on the device without encryption, so do not use a password that you use anywhere else. We cannot recover a forgotten password or a lost account.

## Where your data is stored
- Windows (Microsoft Store or download): in your user folder, under AppData\Roaming\Godot\app_userdata\C.L.E.D
- Linux: in your home folder, under .local/share/Godot/app_userdata/C.L.E.D
- Web version: in your browser's storage for the itch.io website. The itch.io website is run by itch.io and follows its own privacy policy.

## Removing your data
- Settings → Delete My Account (while logged in) permanently removes your account, progress, stars and Simulation scores.
- Settings on the Login screen → Delete All Data removes every account and all progress on the device and resets all settings.
- Microsoft Store version: uninstall CLID from the Start menu (right-click → Uninstall). Use Delete All Data first if you also want your saved data gone.

## Younger players
If you are under 18, please play with the guidance of a parent, guardian or teacher.
